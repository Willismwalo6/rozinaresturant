Our Story

Hospitality on Every Plate

Rozina's is a multi-cuisine restaurant known for generous portions, flavour-first cooking and warm service. Our Nakuru branch at Westside Mall brings together coastal Swahili dishes, Indian tandoor and curries, Italian pizza and pasta, and charcoal grills.

Whether it is a quick burger, a family feast or a table for a celebration, we want every visit to feel unhurried and welcoming.
