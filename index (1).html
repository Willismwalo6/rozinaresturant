Our Story

Where Family Meals Become Occasions

Rozina's was built around a simple idea: great food brings people together. Our Nakuru restaurant welcomes families for Sunday lunches, friends for long catch-ups, colleagues for business lunches and groups celebrating birthdays and milestones.

Every plate, from a slow-cooked biryani to a sizzling mixed grill, is made to be shared. Pull up a chair, order a little of everything and stay a while.
